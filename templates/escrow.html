How escrow works

1
You request

A traveler accepts your local or online-store request.

2
You pay escrow

Abroad holds the item total and traveler fee.

3
Traveler buys

They upload a receipt within 2 days or you are refunded.

4
Admin verifies

The receipt is checked before the order moves forward.

5
You release

After delivery and your release code, the traveler is paid.
